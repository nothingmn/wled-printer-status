The limit on changeable words why languages are not free

What the font draws today43 glyphs — A–Z, 0–9, % . - _ / and space
Accented Latin — É, Ü, Å, Øfeasible: about thirty more 5×7 glyphs
Greek, Cyrillicfeasible, but a second alphabet rather than a tweak
Chinese, Japanese, Koreannot at 5×7 — a legible glyph needs 12×12 and up
Type a character the font does not have and the preview leaves a gap and says so, because that is exactly what the panel would do. Any word made of A–Z and digits works today; accented characters are a small piece of work; CJK needs a different font and a much wider glyph budget.
