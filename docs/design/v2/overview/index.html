What a one-click bind does
  • the whole light — no segment, unless the light has several and you pick one later
  • brightness follows your day schedule if you have one, otherwise 128
  • colours from your table, untouched — printing green, waiting amber, error red
  • a panel gets the bar, the percentage and the job name; a strip gets the fill
  • events — the finished word on a panel, its colour on a strip; the rest off until you turn them on
Pick a printer, then press bind on a light — or bind in order to pair everything still unbound. The same printer may sit on several lights; a second room is the same one click.
